Eye & Pupil Tracking

A developing computer-vision prototype aimed at locally manufacturable Iranian eye- and pupil-tracking devices. The work connects camera optics, eye localization, and time-indexed measurements while the hardware and validation process continue to evolve.

Abstract

This project explores a locally manufacturable eye- and pupil-tracking device suited to development in Iran. The aim is to connect accessible camera and optical components with a computer-vision pipeline that locates the eye, estimates pupil position, and produces time-indexed measurements. Existing code experiments cover eye-region extraction, image enhancement, video processing, and pupil or iris localization.

The project is undergoing further development. Hardware integration, calibration, and validation against independently annotated recordings remain active parts of the work. The single figure is a concept rendering of the intended optical prototype and tracking workflow; it is presented as a design direction rather than an experimental result.
