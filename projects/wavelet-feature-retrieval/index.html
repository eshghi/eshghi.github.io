Wavelet Retrieval Experiments

An early content-based image-retrieval study using multiscale wavelet representations and distance-based ranking. This 2023 work established the initial CBIR direction and led to a paper published in Iran.

Abstract

This 2023 study was an early exploration of content-based image retrieval. It examined whether multiscale wavelet information could provide a useful representation for comparing a query image with a visual collection. The workflow decomposes an image into frequency and orientation components, converts those components into a descriptor, and ranks candidates by distance to the query.

The experiment established the first CBIR direction that later supported a paper published in Iran. Its value here is historical and methodological: it documents the progression from explicit image transformations to ranked visual search. The single illustration summarizes that process without presenting invented benchmark values or simulated performance claims.
