Image Reconstruction & Ranking

A 2026 course coding study of image reconstruction, structural similarity, and pairwise ranking. The notebook records training behavior, reconstructed examples, and paired inputs as a compact implementation exercise.

Abstract

This 2026 course project is a compact coding study rather than a claim of a completed research contribution. It explores how a neural reconstruction model preserves visual information and how paired images can be represented for a ranking task. The notebook prepares the image data, trains the reconstruction component, records its learning curves, and saves side-by-side examples for qualitative inspection. It then extends the exercise to structural similarity and pairwise inputs.

The resulting record is useful as evidence of implementation practice: it connects model training with outputs that can be inspected directly. The gallery therefore focuses on the saved reconstruction examples, training behavior, and a paired-image sample. No benchmark claim or publication status is attached to the exercise.
